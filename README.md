# Balatro-Vita Modding Guide

A guide to simple modding for the game **Balatro** on the **PSVita**.

---

## Requirements

- [Balatro-Vita](https://github.com/Daexxxx/Balatro-Vita)  
- [rePatch reLoaded](https://github.com/SonicMastr/rePatch-reLoaded)  
- WinRAR or any archive program that can unzip and create ZIP files

---

## Steps

### **Step 1:**  
If you don't have the game installed, do that first.  
Installation instructions are available on the [GitHub page for the port](https://github.com/Daexxxx/Balatro-Vita).

---

### **Step 2:**  
Take the `game.love` file and rename it to `game.love.zip`.

![Rename game.love to game.love.zip](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/91f2fc20-9298-4ccf-938c-b242db7e097b)

---

### **Step 3:** 
Extract the `zip` you should have a folder called `game.love`

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1b213665-aea5-4753-9e57-980554c86df8)

---

### **Step 4:** 
Connect to your PSVita with  `USB ` or  `FTP `
Go to `/ux0:/app/BALA00001`

---

### **Step 5:** 
Remove the `game.love` from your Vita!

Place the new `game.love` Folder that you created on your pc before on your Vita

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/668a000c-049e-46b5-9b1a-01fe2beb0552)

---

### **Step 5:**
We are almost done for setting up

Go Back to your Ux0 folder and Look for the Folder called `rePatch` if you just installed rePatch and its not there create the folder

---

### **Step 6:**
Creat a folder called `BALA00001` this is the folder where all the files for the mods will be  

![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/ee3f7e12-d7fb-4945-81db-438b795d324e)

And thats pretty much it While you can not run mods like cryptid you can replace Texttures Music Text etc.

This method maybe breaks in the future will update if that happens
